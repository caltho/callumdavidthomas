Y2K × Basement

"oxidised chrome, after midnight" — the Wipeout UI, left running in a dark room for 25 years.

The crossover isn't cosplay. In 1995 The Designers Republic built the Wipeout interface and the visual identity of Warp Records — peak Y2K cyber graphics and underground techno were one scene. This rebuilds that overlap, rendered in your existing basement palette. Two mockups below: the maximal idea, and the restrained production version. Pick where on the dial you want to sit.

The restraint budget (what keeps it "complex yet subtle"):

max 1 holographic element per viewport · pixel font only in chrome, never body · 1 cold accent (phosphor) at LED scale only · CRT scanline subtle & toggle-able ("CALM" in the taskbar) · popups are dismissible non-blocking toasts, never blocking, never scroll-hijacking · all motion gated by prefers-reduced-motion.
